# Code of conduct

Select is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want that to stay pleasant for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, credit others, and welcome newcomers' questions.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions, chat channels and events connected to the project.

**Reporting:** write to the maintainers at the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` (use "conduct" in the subject). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude people who break these rules.

These rules are inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
